Zillow → Pipeline Bookmarklet

One click on any Zillow listing sends it straight to your pipeline. Nothing to install in Chrome settings.

JavaScript is required to generate the bookmarklet link.

Step 1 — Drag this button to your bookmarks bar
(If the bar isn't visible: Ctrl+Shift+B on Windows / ⌘+Shift+B on Mac)

Save to Pipeline
☝ drag this button to your bookmarks bar
  • 2

    Browse Zillow as normal

    Open any listing detail page — the URL will look like
    zillow.com/homedetails/…/12345_zpid

  • 3

    Click your bookmarklet

    A panel slides in from the bottom-right. It captures every field and up to 50 photos, then shows a green confirmation. Done.

  • 4

    Review in the admin pipeline

    Open admin pipeline to review, score, and publish the listing.

Why this works better than the extension

  • No Chrome settings to fiddle with — just drag one link
  • Fixes happen instantly on Replit — your bookmarklet auto-updates with no reinstall
  • Works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave, Arc — any desktop browser
  • Reads Zillow's own page data — never gets blocked or rate-limited
  • Captures all fields: address, rent, beds/baths, photos (up to 50), amenities, pet policy, and more

The script is hosted on Replit so it can be updated and debugged at any time.

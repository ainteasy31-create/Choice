Help Center

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about renting through Choice Properties.

How do I apply for a property?
Click "Apply Now" on any listing. The secure online application takes about 15 minutes — you'll need your ID, proof of income, and basic rental history. Nothing is charged at submission. After you submit, our leasing team contacts you within 24 hours to securely complete the $50 application fee. Your application is activated for review only after payment is completed — and applicants who complete payment quickly are placed earlier in the review queue.
How long does approval take?
Applications are typically processed within 24 to 72 hours after the application fee payment is completed. Faster decisions are often made for applicants who provide accurate information and remain responsive — and applicants who act quickly are often prioritized in the review process. You'll receive email updates at every stage and can track your status anytime using your Application ID.
What do I need to qualify?
Generally: income of 3× monthly rent, no prior evictions, and a credit check. Specific requirements vary by property and landlord. Contact us if you have questions about your situation.
Is the application fee refundable?
The application fee is a flat $50 and covers your background and credit screening. It is non-refundable once payment has been processed. If your application is not approved, you may be eligible for application credits toward a future application — see our Application Credit Policy for details.
How do I track my application?
Use the Application Tracker with your Application ID (emailed when you applied). You can also reach us at 707-706-3137 or support@choiceproperties.com.
Can I apply for multiple properties?
Yes. You may apply to multiple listings simultaneously. Each application requires a separate $50 fee since each involves a separate background and credit check.
Are pets allowed?
Pet policies vary by property. Look for the "Pets OK" badge on listing cards, or filter by "Pet Friendly" on the listings page. Contact us with specific questions about a property.
Still have questions?
Call or text us at 707-706-3137 or email support@choiceproperties.com. We respond same day.

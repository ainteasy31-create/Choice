Trusted Property Management Services in Kansas, Florida, and Missouri

CJ Real Estate, Inc. strives to provide a high level of customer service at an affordable price. We keep our costs low so we can pass those savings on to our customers in the form of lower commissions.  Our system is continually evolving with modern technology geared to provide our owners and tenants with the latest in property management innovations.

CJ Real Estate, Inc. is a full-service property management company specializing in investment residential real estate. We have spent years developing methods and systems to keep owner costs low, vacancy rates low and profits high and we are constantly pursuing new methods and technology to keep ahead of our competition. In addition, we have a sizable investor base constantly looking for investment acquisitions.

CJ Real Estate, Inc. provides sales and management services in Missouri and Kansas, as well as the Southern Florida Gulf Coast.  We have offices located in:

Blue Springs, Mo

St Louis, Mo

Columbia Mo

Tampa Florida.

“New” Interested in Buying or Selling Real Estate?

We have been a real estate brokerage since 2000 and have extensive experience in real estate sales and acquisitions.  We have agents available to help you in your real estate needs.

Owner/Investors: If you are an owner looking to sell, our agents can be of assistance as well as we can market your properties to our over 1000 active investment owners to see if they want to add to their existing portfolio.  If you are looking to buy additional properties, our buyer agents can be of assistance.

Tenants: If you are a tenant looking to move up to buy your own new home, we have agents that can help you as well.

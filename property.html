Before You Apply
Here's exactly what to expect.
Application Required Before Viewing. This property operates on an application-first basis. Viewings are arranged after an application has been submitted and approved — not before. Applications are reviewed and a decision is made before any property access is scheduled.
The Process — Step by Step
1
Apply Online
Select this property and submit your application. Nothing is charged at submission — your application is received and placed in queue once the application fee is paid.
Free to submit
2
Pay the Application Fee
The application fee activates your application for review. Payment must be completed before your application enters the queue — paying promptly places you earlier in the review order.
$50 — non-refundable once processed
3
Active Review (24–72 Hours)
Your application — including background and credit check — is reviewed. Decisions are typically made within 24 to 72 hours of payment. Applicants who respond promptly and provide complete, accurate information are often prioritized. Multiple applications may be reviewed for the same property.
4
Approval / Selection
If selected, you are notified by email. This step is time-sensitive — units are offered on a first-completion basis among approved applicants. You'll need to move to the next step quickly to secure the property.
5
Pay the Holding Deposit
A holding deposit reserves the property and removes it from active availability while your lease is being finalized. You have 24–48 hours to complete this after selection. This is not an extra charge — it converts directly into your move-in costs.
Fully credited toward your move-in costs — not an additional charge
6
Sign Your Lease
You receive your lease documentation electronically, review it, and sign — typically within 48 hours of selection. Your electronic signature carries the same legal weight as a handwritten signature under federal law (ESIGN Act).
7
Move In
Once the lease is fully executed and move-in costs are paid, we coordinate your move-in date with the landlord. Every step is guided and documented from start to finish.
🏠 You're in

You're Protected at Every Step

If Your Application Is Not Approved
You automatically receive 2 application credits — valid for 45 days — that fully waive the application fee on your next two applications, regardless of the fee amount. A denial doesn't cost you your ability to keep looking.
Property Accuracy Guarantee
If the property is found to significantly differ from what was listed — wrong size, missing amenities, unavailable unit, or misrepresented condition — before your lease is finalized, you are eligible for a full refund of your holding deposit. Submit the discrepancy in writing and it is reviewed promptly.
